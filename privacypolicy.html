This Privacy Policy explains how HiddenCalculator collects, uses and protects your information when you use our application.

1. About the App

HiddenCalculator is a calculator application that also provides a private vault feature. Users can access the private vault through a secret PIN.

2. Information We Collect

  • Authentication information processed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Photos, videos, audio and documents selected by users for the vault.

If you use guest mode, your information may remain stored locally on your device without account-based cloud backup.

3. Permissions
