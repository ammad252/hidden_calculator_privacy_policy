3. Permissions

  • Photos, videos and audio (media access): used only to let you choose files to put into the vault.
  • Deleting originals: when you add a file to the vault, the app removes the original from your gallery or storage so that it exists only inside the vault. Android may ask you to confirm this. Please keep this in mind before adding files, especially in guest mode where there is no backup.
  • Internet: used for sign-in and for the cloud backup and restore of account users.

4. How we use information

  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your files for advertising or analytics. We cannot view your vault files in readable form.

6. Data storage and security

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your PIN and account password private.
